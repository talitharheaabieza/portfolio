Thompson Sampling Ad Creative Optimization (XYZ Coffee)

Jan - Apr 2026

Project Overview

This project explores real-time ad creative optimization using a Thompson Sampling multi-armed bandit algorithm. Unlike traditional static A/B testing, which can waste traffic on underperforming creatives, Thompson Sampling dynamically shifts traffic toward winning variants while balancing the need for continued exploration.

Core Objectives

  • Maximize real-time click-through rates (CTR) and conversion volume.
  • Identify the root statistical cause behind creative performance differences.
  • Reduce the "cost of exploration" inherent in standard experimental design.

Key Methodologies

Bandit Algorithm
Adaptive Traffic

Implemented Thompson Sampling to dynamically shift impressions toward higher-performing creatives based on Bayesian probability.

Regression
Component Analysis

Utilized Python to deconstruct creatives, analyzing individual elements (motion vs. static, discount badge) as independent predictors.

Findings
Motion Hurts CTR

Regression revealed that animated elements actually reduced CTR (p<0.01), as movement distracted from the primary offer.

Visualizations

Traffic Allocation: The algorithm quickly learns to prefer Variant A (winning creative) over Variants B, C, and D.
Comparison: Traditional A/B test splits traffic evenly (25% each), incurring high regret costs.

CRM Analytics: Modeling Missed Conversions and Churn Indicators (Duolingo)

Jan - Mar 2026

Project Overview

This project analyzed a simulated CRM database modeled after Duolingo's freemium subscription business, combining user profiles, lesson activity logs, marketing email interaction data, and transactional history. The goal was to identify critical engagement drop-off points for free users and early churn indicators for paid subscribers.

Key Analytical Focus Areas

  • Conversion Thresholds: Modeled upgrade likelihood based on lesson frequency and streak duration using complex SQL joins and window functions.
  • Missed Conversions: Identified "highly engaged but non-converting" free users by querying lesson activity against purchase logs.
  • Churn Prediction: Modeled subscription renewal behavior across different tiers (Super vs. Max) and payment cadence (Auto-renew vs. manual).

Market Basket Analysis (XYZ Restaurant, Indonesia)

Aug - Dec 2025

Project Overview

Conducted association rule mining in R on transactional sales data from a local Indonesian F&B chain to evaluate item co-occurrences across different dayparts (noon vs. night) and optimize menu engineering.

Key Insights & Applications

  • Temporal Sales Spikes: Sales peaks align heavily around 14:00 (lunch) and 20:00 (dinner), establishing clear bounds for daypart-specific promotions.
  • Noon Strategy: High association between heavy meals and non-coffee beverages (e.g., Fried Rice + Mineral Water).
  • Night Strategy: Shift toward indulgence and social pairing, showing strong lift between desserts and coffee (e.g., Chocolate Cheese Fried Banana + Signature Milk Coffee).

R · NUS Academic Project

Consumer Perception Study: Hawker Centres vs Modern Dining

Aug - Nov 2025

Project Overview

This NUS MSc MAI project evaluated Singapore youth dining preferences between traditional hawker centres and modern options (cafes, restaurants, food courts). I executed a mixed-methods approach combining statistical analysis of survey data in R with sentiment analysis of Focus Group Discussions (FGDs).

Methodologies & Key Stats

  • Quantitative: Factor analysis and Cluster analysis (K-means) performed on N=70 survey responses using RStudio.
  • Qualitative: Thematic analysis conducted on N=5 FGD participants to explore underlying emotional drivers (nostalgia vs. convenience).

Key Findings

Driver 1
Functional Utility

Price accessibility and speed are the primary factors driving hawker visits (p<0.05).

Driver 2
Ambience Friction

Poor ventilation and perceived lower hygiene constitute the major friction points for frequent hawker dining.

Segmentation
"Occasional" Segment

Cluster analysis identified the "Occasional Hawker Diner" as a massive growth opportunity for digitization initiatives.

Run Rate Forecasting Model Refinement

Jan 2023 - Apr 2024

Problem Statement

The previous internal forecasting logic relied heavily on simple linear projections, causing significant variance (up to 30%) during volatile promotional campaign periods or off-peak weekends.

Refinement Strategy

I spearheaded a logic refinement by deconstructing historical sales patterns into three predictive components:

  • Weekday vs. Weekend Seasonality: Modeled unique daily velocity factors across product verticals.
  • Campaign Impact Weights: Formulated historical 'campaign spike' ratios to adjust projections dynamically during known promotion dates.
  • Weighted Recency: Implemented weighted moving averages to prioritize immediate preceding daily performance over distant historic data.

Business Impact

Variance Reduction
30% → 10%

Reduced the error gap of sales projections by factor of 3 org-wide.

Commercial Planning
Optimized AOV

Improved accuracy allowed commercial teams to optimize AOV-enhancing bundles during forecast off-peak periods.

Stakeholder Trust
Methodology Shift

Successfully migrated commercial teams from reactive linear modeling to proactive seasonal forecasting.
